Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to KamKaj.

This Privacy Policy explains how KamKaj collects, uses, protects and handles information when you use our application.

1. Introduction

KamKaj ("KamKaj", "we", "us", or "our") operates a mobile application designed to connect people looking for work with employers looking for workers.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use KamKaj, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, where provided
  • Profile information
  • Job-related information
  • Location information provided by the user
  • Job posting information
  • Profile or verification images uploaded by the user
  • Information required to operate and secure user accounts
  • Information voluntarily provided through the application

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide job-search and job-posting services.
  • Connect employers with potential workers.
  • Display relevant job opportunities.
  • Send job notifications and alerts.
  • Improve application functionality and performance.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain the security of our services.
  • Respond to user requests and support inquiries.

4. Location Information

KamKaj may use location-related information to help users discover jobs relevant to their area.

Users may also provide a location manually when creating or viewing job information.

Location information is used for purposes related to the services provided by KamKaj.

5. Photos and Images

KamKaj may allow users to upload profile photographs, verification documents or other images related to their use of the service.

Users should only upload information that they are authorized to provide.

6. Job Information

When an employer creates a job listing, information provided in the listing may be visible to other KamKaj users.

Users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive or private information in publicly visible job listings.

7. Communication

KamKaj may provide features that allow users to contact employers or job seekers directly, including phone calls or external messaging services.

When users choose to contact another user through an external service, that communication may be subject to the privacy policy and terms of that external service.

8. Service Providers

KamKaj may use third-party service providers to help operate the application, including services for database hosting, authentication, notifications, infrastructure and application functionality.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through KamKaj against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

11. Account Deletion

Requesting account deletion

Users may request deletion of their KamKaj account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, contact us using the email address listed below and include enough information for us to identify your account.

Some information may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or maintain legitimate business records.

12. Children's Privacy

KamKaj is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
