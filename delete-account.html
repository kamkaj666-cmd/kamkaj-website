How the deletion process works

1
Submit your request

Send us an account deletion request using the button above.

2
Account verification

We may verify that the request belongs to the account owner before processing the deletion.

3
Account deletion

Once verified, your KamKaj account and associated personal data will be deleted, subject to any information we are legally required to retain.

What data will be deleted?

Depending on the information associated with your account, deletion may include:

  • Your account and profile information
  • Your phone number or other account information
  • Job posts associated with your account
  • Profile images and uploaded content
  • Other personal information associated with your account
Important: Certain information may be retained when required for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes. Any applicable retention is handled according to our privacy policy.
